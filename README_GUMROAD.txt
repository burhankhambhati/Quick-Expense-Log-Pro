------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRO & GAME-CHANGER FEATURES INCLUDED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SMART UPI & BANK SMS AUTO-PARSER:
   Paste any transaction SMS or payment confirmation (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm,
   HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.) to instantly extract amount, merchant, and category.

2. VOICE EXPENSE DICTATION (🎙️ SPEAK TO LOG):
   Tap the microphone and dictate naturally (e.g., "Chai 30" or "Petrol 500").
   The app automatically detects the description and amount!

3. DYNAMIC DAILY SAFE-TO-SPEND TARGET:
   Instantly calculates how much you can safely spend today based on your
   remaining monthly budget divided by remaining days in the month.

4. 50/30/20 WEALTH RULE & ANALYTICS:
   Visual breakdown of your expenses across Needs (50%), Wants (30%), and
   Savings (20%), plus financial health score and monthly category charts.

5. HISAB KHATA (UDHAR LEDGER) + WHATSAPP REMINDERS:
   Track money lent ("Maine Diye") or borrowed ("Maine Liye") with 1-click
   pre-formatted WhatsApp payment reminder messages and instant debt settlement.

6. RECURRING SUBSCRIPTIONS ("1-CLICK PAY & LOG"):
   Track recurring commitments (Wi-Fi, Netflix, Rent, Gym, Maid, Milk) with
   due date countdowns and 1-click expense logging.

7. INDIAN CASH NOTE COUNTER (ROJMEL):
   Count physical currency notes (₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10, ₹5, ₹2/₹1)
   with live grand total, Indian words conversion, and 1-click expense sync.

8. 1-CLICK BACKUP & EXPORT:
   Export your entire financial history to CSV (Excel) or download an encrypted
   JSON backup file to transfer your data between devices anytime.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO RUN & INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip QuickExpenseLog_App.zip.
2. Double-click "index.html" (or "expense_tracker.html") to open in any browser
   (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Brave, Firefox).
3. To install to phone home screen:
   - Android (Chrome): Tap menu (⋮) > "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
   - iPhone (Safari): Tap Share icon (⎋) > "Add to Home Screen" (+).

Enjoy clean, private, lightning-fast financial tracking!
